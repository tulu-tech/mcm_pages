Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you come to my area?

We currently serve the Bay Area including San Jose, Santa Clara, Sunnyvale, Cupertino, Mountain View, Palo Alto, Fremont, Pleasanton, Dublin, Walnut Creek, San Francisco, Daly City, and surrounding communities. If you're nearby, request a visit and we'll confirm availability.

Can I book for a group or event?

Absolutely. The mobile showroom is designed for both private demos and group events. Workplaces, communities, churches, retirement communities, and neighborhood groups can all request a visit.

Can two people try it together?

Yes. The mobile showroom features multiple chairs so couples, partners, or colleagues can experience the chairs side by side and compare together.

What if I'm still comparing options?

That's exactly what the demo is for. Our specialists will help you understand the meaningful differences between models — comfort, features, fit, and value — without pressure. You walk away more informed.

Can I request a workplace or community stop?

Yes. HR teams, office managers, community organizers, and residential managers can request the truck as a wellness activation, employee appreciation event, or community pop-up.

How long does a demo take?

A private demo typically takes 20–30 minutes, though you're welcome to take your time. Event activations can run for several hours depending on the arrangement.

Is there any obligation to buy?

None at all. The mobile showroom is a zero-pressure experience. Try the chairs, ask questions, and take your time. We're here to help you decide with confidence — on your own timeline.
