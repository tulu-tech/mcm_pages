Technology Guide

What Makes a Premium Massage Chair Different

Understanding the key technologies helps you make a smarter decision in the showroom. Here's what you'll experience — and what each feature actually does for your body.

Premium Standard
🤲

4D Massage Arms

4D massage adds a fourth dimension to roller movement — variable speed and depth in addition to the standard three axes. The result is a massage that genuinely mimics human hands, with nuanced pressure variation that feels intuitive and therapeutic.

Spinal Relief
🚀

Zero Gravity Recline

Inspired by NASA, zero gravity positioning elevates your legs above your heart, decompressing the spine and distributing body weight evenly. This posture maximizes every massage stroke's effectiveness and is especially beneficial for lower back pain relief.

Personalization
📡

Automatic Body Scan

Before every session, premium chairs scan your body's exact dimensions — shoulder width, spine curvature, seated height — and map the massage rollers precisely to your anatomy. No manual adjustment required. Your massage is calibrated for you, every time.

Full Body
💨

Air Compression

Strategically placed air cells around the shoulders, arms, calves, and feet inflate and deflate in rhythmic sequences — mimicking the compression and stretch of a professional sports massage. Highly effective for circulation and muscle recovery.

Therapeutic
🔥

Heat Therapy

Targeted heat in the lumbar region relaxes deep muscle tissue before the massage begins, amplifying the effectiveness of every roller stroke. Some models include heating in the calves and feet as well — essential for tension-prone users.

Coverage
📐

L-Track vs. S-Track

An S-track follows your spine's natural curve from neck to lower back. An L-track extends further — curving under the seat to cover the glutes and upper hamstrings. For those with sciatica, hip tension, or lower body tightness, L-track is a meaningful upgrade.
