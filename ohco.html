OHCO Technology

The Engineering Behind the Experience

Every OHCO innovation exists for one reason: to make the massage feel more real, more personal, and more restorative than anything you've experienced in a chair.

Sens8 Massage Mechanism

The heart of every OHCO chair. The Sens8 is a 4D massage engine that uses advanced algorithms and precision motor control to create nuanced "feel" variations — changes in speed, depth, and pressure landing that mimic the instinctive adjustments of a master shiatsu therapist. This is not a simple roller with speed settings. It's a responsive system that delivers a different sensation on every pass.

MaxTrack (49" L-Track)

A 49-inch ultra-long massage track that follows your body's natural S-curve from the base of your neck all the way down through your glutes. MaxTrack maintains consistent roller contact through every recline position, so there are never "dead zones" where the massage loses you. This is critical for buyers who carry tension in their lower back and hips.

Arms of Embrace + Easy Access Doors

The signature OHCO enclosure design — rear swinging doors that open from either side for effortless entry and exit, then lock securely when reclined. Combined with "Arms of Embrace" side panels, the chair creates a cocoon-like experience that feels private, supportive, and immersive. This is the design detail that distinguishes OHCO from open-frame competitors.

Complete Sensory Immersion

OHCO goes beyond massage with a full sensory environment: Bluetooth audio immersion so you can layer music or guided meditation, aromatherapy diffusion for scent-based relaxation, an air ionizer for fresher ambient air, and chromotherapy lighting that creates a calming visual atmosphere. Together, these transform a massage session into a complete wellness ritual.
