OHCO Technology

OHCO Technology: Sens8, MaxTrack & Movesync

Every OHCO feature exists for one reason: to make each session feel more layered, more personal, and more useful as part of a daily recovery routine.

Sens8 Massage Mechanism

The heart of every OHCO chair. The Sens8 is a 4D massage engine that uses advanced algorithms and precision motor control to create nuanced "feel" variations — changes in speed, depth, and pressure landing that mimic the instinctive adjustments of a master shiatsu therapist. This is not a simple roller with speed settings. It's a responsive system that delivers a different sensation on every pass.

MaxTrack (49" L-Track)

A 49-inch ultra-long massage track that follows your body's natural S-curve from the base of your neck all the way down through your glutes. MaxTrack maintains consistent roller contact through every recline position, so there are never "dead zones" where the massage loses you. This is critical for buyers who carry tension in their lower back and hips.

Movesync Dual Massage Mechanism

The Movesync dual massage mechanism is one of the biggest reasons to compare the M8 Neo and M8 Neo LE in person. Instead of relying on a single roller path, the M8 platform layers back, neck, shoulder, and sensory features into a more dimensional session that single-system chairs cannot match.

Complete Sensory Environment

OHCO goes beyond massage with a complete sensory environment. Bluetooth Audio Immersion lets you connect your smart device to play music, podcasts, calming soundscapes, or guided meditation during your session. Aromatherapy adds scent-based relaxation, while the air ionizer supports a fresher ambient feel. The M-Sense Light Pool creates a soft glow around the chair, helping the experience feel more like a daily ritual than a routine.

Lower-Leg Comfort

Calf, Foot & Lower-Leg Massage

TheraElliptical calf kneading is designed around coordinated compression and rotation, gently compressing and kneading the sides of the calves while rear calf rollers work along the back of the lower leg. The movement is inspired by how a shiatsu master gently compresses, releases, and works through the lower leg, leaving your calves refreshed without relying on simple squeezing alone.

The coordinated compression and roller movement can support a lower-leg session that feels focused, layered, and leaves your calves feeling relaxed, supported, and ready for the rest of the day.
