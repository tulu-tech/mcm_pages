Is the OHCO M8 NEO better than the Panasonic MAN1?

It depends. OHCO is better for immersive luxury and design-led relaxation. Panasonic is better for precision, body scanning, and targeted massage performance. Try both before deciding.

Is the Panasonic MAN1 better for deep massage?

Panasonic MAN1 is often preferred by customers who want a more technical and targeted massage feel, especially because of Real Pro Ultra H.I., pressure sensing, and Junetsu Ultra Kneading. A side-by-side in-person demo helps you judge whether the intensity feels comfortable.

Which chair is better for neck and shoulders?

Which chair is more luxurious?

OHCO M8 NEO is the more design-led, immersive luxury option because its experience includes craftsmanship, Easy Access Doors, Arms of Embrace, light, sound, heat, aromatherapy, and a stronger furniture-like presence.

Panasonic MAN1 is the more precision-engineered option thanks to Real Pro Ultra H.I., 1260P Acupoint Body Scan, pressure-sensing rollers, SL-Track coverage, infrared-heated rollers, and detailed customization.
