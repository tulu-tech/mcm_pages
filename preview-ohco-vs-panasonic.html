Quick Verdict

Start with the kind of experience you want.

Choose OHCO if you want a luxury chair that feels like a design object, with Japanese craftsmanship, shiatsu-inspired choreography, Easy Access Doors, Arms of Embrace, MaxTrack, total-body heat, air ionizer, aromatherapy, chromotherapy, and a sensory wellness experience.

Heat and programs

Useful for daily unwinding, not a medical promise.
